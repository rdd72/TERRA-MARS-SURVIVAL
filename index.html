MARS-X

SURVIVAL AT JEZERO-7 HABITAT
5 SOLS · 1 ALIEN · 0 SIGNAL FROM EARTH

Walk the base from side to side, search the furniture with your own hands and repair the MARS-X sabotages… quietly.

Goal: survive 5 Martian days (~80 s each). If the CRISIS bar reaches 0, you lose.

Move: hold the ◀ ▶ buttons, use A/D or ←/→, or click the floor to walk there.
Interact [E]: stand in front of a door, a workstation or a piece of furniture and press E (or the ✋ button, or click it).
Furniture close-ups: the Cabinet, Shelf and Jars open a first-person view. Click an item to inspect it, click it again (or press ✋ Take) to grab it. Beware of look-alike junk.
Repairs: each sabotage needs one spare part that is always stored in another room (the Lab manual tells you which). Carry the part to the broken station and press E: it is used automatically.
Flashlight [F] / Recharge [R]: during blackouts and every night the base goes dark. The flashlight drains while it is on in the dark. Find 🔋 batteries inside furniture and recharge, or you will be left blind.
MARS-X patrols: the motion tracker on the map shows it when it is next door. If it walks into your room: FREEZE (do not move or work) until it leaves, or run for a door early.
Chase: if it detects you, or NOISE hits 100. [H] hide: hold SPACE. [F] flee: type the arrow sequence. P = pause.
